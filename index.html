HELP & GUIDANCE

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on tracking, privacy, deadlines, and verification procedures.

How do I track my complaint without logging in?
Every report generates a unique, non-guessable Tracking ID (e.g., CR-2026-8492). You can paste this code into the "Track Complaint" search bar anytime on desktop or mobile to view full real-time progress and photos.
What happens if a field team marks a complaint resolved but the problem isn't fixed?
You have the guaranteed right to Reopen the complaint within 7 days of resolution. When reopened, the case is automatically escalated with High priority to the Senior Grievance Officer and the previous field worker's department head.
Is my exact home address or phone number visible to the public?
No. Your privacy is strictly protected. Public incident boards only display general ward and landmark information. Personal contact numbers and exact residential details are stored encrypted and accessible only to authorized municipal field officers for dispatch clarification.
How does the AI Duplicate Detection Agent work?
When you enter your complaint description and location, our AI Duplicate Detection Agent compares geographical coordinates, category, and keyword semantics against existing open reports. If a match is found, you can 1-click "Support Existing Report" to boost its priority instead of creating redundant paperwork.
What does the prototype disclaimer mean?
CivicResolve AI is an independent civic technology research prototype engineered to demonstrate next-generation transparent governance. Dispatches and status workflows are simulated within this application environment unless an authorized municipal API is integrated.
